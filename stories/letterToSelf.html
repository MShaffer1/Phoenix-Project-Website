Love Letter to Self: A Reminder of Your Strength, Worth, and Resilience

Published on August 18, 2026 • Written by Michelle Shaffer

Dear Self, You are truly loved. You're amazing and perfect just the way you are. You are intelligent, courageous, strong in your beliefs, and resilient; able to take on any challenge.

You love unconditionally with every fiber of your soul. You are thoughtful, considerate, and compassionate.

"Remember, too, that you are not your body, you are not your mind, you are not your thoughts or feelings."

You have all these things, yes, and they are uniquely yours to take care of, cherish, and love. Who you are is not dependent on what others think of you, nor are you responsible for how they feel about you. Be you, because that's all you need to be.

You are worthy. You are loved. You are beautiful. You are strong. You are brave. You are capable. You are resilient. You are intelligent. You are courageous. You are amazing and perfect just the way you are.
