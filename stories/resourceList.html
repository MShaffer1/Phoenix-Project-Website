Support Resources & Helplines

A curated directory of trusted support networks, national helplines, specialized care programs, and financial assistance.

National Helplines & Immediate Support

  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 (Available 24/7, free, and confidential in the US and Canada).
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a crisis counselor 24/7.
  • American Cancer Society: Call 1-800-227-2345 for 24/7 support, lodging, and local rides.
  • National Cancer Institute: Access evidence-based cancer information, clinical trial searches, and live chat/phone support via 1-800-4-CANCER.
  • CancerCare: Call 1-800-813-HOPE for free professional counseling from oncology social workers and telephone support groups.
  • Cancer Support Community: Access virtual networking, distress screening, online support groups, and helpline support (1-888-793-9355).
  • Joe's House: A nationwide non-profit housing directory helping cancer patients and caregivers find discounted lodging near treatment centers.

Survivorship & Whole-Person Care

  • Anticancer Lifestyle Program: A non-profit program explicitly designed to fill the "what now?" gap after primary cancer treatment.
  • Anticancer Lifestyle Allies Network: A network of non-profit organizations taking a holistic, whole-person approach to cancer survivorship.
  • American Institute for Cancer Research (AICR): Evidence-based guidelines on nutrition, physical activity, and lifestyle factors during and after cancer treatment.

Specialized & Targeted Breast Cancer Support

  • Sharsheret: Personalized support, educational resources, peer connection, and tailored kits for young women and families facing breast and ovarian cancer.
  • Living Beyond Breast Cancer (LBBC): Tailored programs, emotional support, and trusted guidance for all stages of breast cancer, including metastatic diagnosis.
  • United Breast Cancer Foundation (UBCF): Direct financial support, patient advocacy, screening, and holistic wellness grants for patients and families affected by breast cancer.
  • Young Survival Coalition (YSC): Dedicated specifically to young adults diagnosed with breast cancer under age 40, offering peer matching and face-to-face support networks.
  • SHARE Cancer Support: Helplines, webinars, and peer support led by women who have personal experience with breast, ovarian, uterine, or cervical cancer.

Legal, Navigation & Practical Rights

  • Triage Cancer: Essential education and free direct navigation on legal, employment, disability, and health insurance issues after a diagnosis.
  • LegalHealth Cancer Care Initiative: Free, specialized legal assistance addressing housing, public benefits, employment rights, and estate planning for cancer patients.
  • Cancer Legal Resource Center (CLRC): A national program by the Disability Rights Legal Center providing confidential information and resources on cancer-related legal issues.
  • LawHelp.org: A nationwide portal connecting low- and moderate-income individuals with free local legal aid programs, self-help materials, and rights guides.
  • OncoLink Legal & Employment Directory: Comprehensive guides and resource directories covering employment protections, FMLA, insurance rights, and legal advocacy during treatment.
  • Cancer and Careers: Practical tools, resume review services, and legal educational resources for managing a career during and after cancer treatment.
  • Job Accommodation Network (JAN): Free expert guidance on workplace accommodations under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) during treatment and return-to-work phases.

Financial & Practical Aid

  • A Helping Hand Database by CancerCare: Search co-pay assistance, travel grants, and utility help by zip code and diagnosis.
  • United Way: Visit the website or dial 211 for local emergency assistance, housing, and essential community services.
  • Patient Advocate Foundation (PAF): Case management and financial aid funds to help underinsured patients resolve insurance, debt, and access-to-care barriers.
  • HealthWell Foundation: Financial assistance for underinsured patients to help cover copays, premiums, deductibles, and pediatric therapy costs.
